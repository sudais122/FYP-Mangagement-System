Chairman’s Message

A defining phase of your academic journey

Dear Students,

The Final Year Project represents a defining phase of your academic journey. It reflects your analytical thinking, discipline, and professional readiness.

I encourage you to select projects that address real-world challenges and demonstrate innovation and responsibility.

Our faculty is fully committed to supporting you throughout this process. Maintain consistency, communicate effectively, and adhere to academic timelines.

Warm regards,
Dr. Nadeem Iqbal
Head, Department of Computer Science
AA

Dr. Aftab Ahmad

FYP Coordinator
Coordinator’s Message

From theory to practice — make it count

Dear Students,

Your Final Year Project is an opportunity to apply theoretical knowledge in a practical environment and demonstrate your capabilities as a future professional.

Choose your project topic wisely, remain consistent in your efforts, and seek guidance whenever necessary.

Discipline, proper documentation, and timely submissions are key to success.

Best wishes,
Dr. Aftab Ahmad
FYP Coordinator
