About the System

Everything You Need, In One Place

Students can access structured information related to project proposals, submission deadlines, evaluations, and academic resources. Stay updated with announcements and ensure all submissions are completed on time.

Project Proposals

Submit and track your FYP proposal through our streamlined digital workflow. Get real-time status updates and supervisor feedback.

Submission Deadlines

Never miss an important date. View all upcoming deadlines for proposals, progress reports, and final submissions in one calendar view.
