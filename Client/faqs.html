Final Year Project — FAQs

Answers to the most common questions students ask about FYP registration, proposals, supervision, and submission.

1

Coursera Courses

Both options are valid pathways and depend on your academic goals. An internship provides real-world industry exposure and professional networking opportunities, while Coursera courses offer structured, certificate-based learning in specialized technical domains. Students unable to secure an eligible internship within the department's approved list are typically advised to pursue Coursera courses to maintain their academic timeline.
Registration is handled through the university's institutional Coursera for Campus portal. Log in with your student credentials, select the department-approved courses, and keep the registration confirmation for submission to the FYP Coordinator. The list of approved courses is published each semester on this portal.
If your campus does not appear in the Coursera for Campus portal, contact the FYP Coordinator immediately. Alternative access arrangements can be made through the department's institutional account, and manual approval of your enrollment will be processed.
Once your application is submitted, the FYP Coordinator reviews it and confirms eligibility within 5–7 working days. You will then receive an email with your course assignments, completion deadlines, and any additional instructions.
Students opting for the Coursera pathway are required to complete a minimum of three (3) department-approved courses, each with a valid certificate of completion. Additional courses are encouraged but do not substitute the FYP proposal or implementation components.
2

Starting Your FYP Proposal

You must have completed 90+ credit hours, maintain a minimum CGPA as defined by the department, and have no pending core course failures. Additionally, all group members must meet these requirements individually.
The standard group size is 2 to 3 members. Groups of 4 are allowed only in exceptional cases where the project scope clearly justifies the additional member, subject to the supervisor's and coordinator's written approval.
Project categories — such as Research, Development, Industry-linked, or Social Impact — are outlined in the official FYP handbook. Choose a category that aligns with your team's strengths, your supervisor's expertise, and the real-world relevance of the problem you aim to solve.
3

Proposal Submission & Review

Only PDF files following the official IEEE-based proposal template are accepted. The filename must follow the convention: GroupID_ProjectTitle_Proposal_v1.pdf. Maximum file size is 10 MB.
After submission, proposals are assigned to an internal review committee. You will receive feedback within 10–14 working days. Outcomes typically include: Approved, Approved with Revisions, or Rejected. Rejected proposals may be resubmitted once, after addressing committee feedback.
